01 / CONTEXT

A reason to join,
before a prompt to generate.

An AI tool alone does not explain why someone should participate. The ticket needs to show what people can create and how it connects to the event before asking them to upload a photo.

01

Motivation

Use the event theme and ticket examples to establish context.

02

Expectation

Connect the chosen template, photo preview and generated result.

03

Next action

Carry the journey forward from generation to publishing and sharing.

The following decisions are drawn from the existing design, rather than presented as interview or experiment findings.

02 / ENTRY

Make the invitation personal.

The entry introduces the activity, while the event page expands its theme and participation details. This establishes context before moving into ticket creation.

ENTRY → EVENT PAGE

Connect the event story to creation

The event page explains the activity; the creation flow handles the task. Open the long screen to inspect its full content.

03 / TEMPLATES

Show the possibilities,
then let people choose.

Preview the visual possibilities before asking for a choice. The template screen turns these styles into selectable options.

A template sets expectations

Red, blue, gold, minimal white and iridescent styles offer different expressions of the celebration, making the intended output tangible before upload.

04 / GENERATION

Upload, confirm,
see the result.

Each screen carries a distinct task: choose a photo, inspect its preview, then view the generated ticket. Separating input preparation from the finished result keeps the sequence readable.

05 / SHARING

The result is the start
of a sharing journey.

Publishing, share content and success feedback follow the generated result. The ticket becomes shareable event content, with a clear confirmation that the action is complete.

06 / SUMMARY

A complete journey,
with participation still to validate.

The case covers event entry, ticket templates, photo upload and preview, and publishing and sharing. The available material does not include launch results or user feedback, so design intentions are not presented as measured growth.
