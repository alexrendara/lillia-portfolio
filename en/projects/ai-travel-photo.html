From a playful discovery
to a travel photo tool.

Across the AI guide, Tongcheng app and Harbin campaign, one image-creation flow adapts to different travel contexts.

01 / CONTEXT

Different contexts.
Different reasons to try.

The project began with portraits and photo experiences in the AI guide, expanded into the Tongcheng app, and then joined a Harbin winter campaign. The emphasis shifts from exploration to practical photo improvements and destination-led creation.

01

Guide: explore

Offer portraits, check-in scenes and effects during a visit.

02

App: see the benefit

Explain value through specific tasks such as removing people or improving cloudy photos.

03

Campaign: feel the destination

Connect winter themes, photo locations and related services.

02 / GUIDE PILOT

Start with a reason
to try during a visit.

In the guide, recommendations bring portraits, check-in scenes and effects together. Users see possible outcomes before moving to details and upload, placing generation within an existing visit.

A shared path: discover → understand the effect → upload → generate and view.

03 / MAIN APP

In the main app,
make the benefit concrete.

Home and community entries broaden discovery. With more varied user intent, specific tasks such as removing passers-by and improving cloudy photos make the tools easier to understand.

04 / HARBIN CAMPAIGN

Give a general tool
a sense of destination.

The destination campaign frames its content around winter travel. Theme details build interest, photo locations provide context, and upload, retouching and photography services connect the experience.

05 / OUTCOMES

Three entry points.
One reusable journey.

The common structure is a reason to enter, a choice of experience, photo generation and a result. Each stage changes the motivation and presentation. Existing app metrics offer a signal, but do not establish a design-driven uplift.
