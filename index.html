About Lillia

在研究、结构与视觉
系统之间推进设计

My work starts by listening closely: to people, constraints, invisible routines, and the small moments where a product either earns trust or loses it. From there, I translate complexity into journeys, prototypes, and interface systems that feel considered without feeling over-designed.

我的设计过程从理解真实场景开始：观察人、流程、限制和情绪，再把复杂问题转译为用户旅程、原型和界面系统。 我希望作品既有清晰的逻辑，也保留探索感和轻盈感。
